## 🌍 What is GreenWise?

GreenWise is a mobile app designed to help you make small, daily changes that add up to a big environmental impact. Think of it as your personal eco-coach for electronics—giving you one simple, actionable tip each day to help you:

- **Use your devices more efficiently** (save energy and money)
- **Take better care of your electronics** (make them last longer)
- **Dispose of e-waste responsibly** (protect our environment)
- **Make smarter buying choices** (reduce your environmental footprint)

Instead of overwhelming you with information, GreenWise delivers **one tip per day** that you can actually do. Complete it, build your streak, and watch your sustainable habits grow!

---

## 🌏 Why GreenWise Matters

### The E-Waste Problem
Every year, the world produces **over 53 million tons of electronic waste**—that's like throwing away 1,000 laptops every second! Only **17% of this waste is properly recycled**. The rest ends up in landfills where toxic chemicals leak into soil and water, harming wildlife and communities.

In the Philippines and many developing countries, improper e-waste disposal is a growing crisis due to:
- Lack of awareness about proper disposal
- Limited recycling facilities
- No structured programs to encourage sustainable habits

### Your Impact
Every small action counts:
- **Extending your phone's life by 1 year** prevents mining of precious metals and rare earth minerals
- **Properly recycling one laptop** recovers valuable materials and keeps toxins out of landfills
- **Using energy-saving settings** reduces your carbon footprint and saves money on electricity
- **Buying refurbished devices** cuts manufacturing emissions by up to 80%

GreenWise helps you take these actions—one day at a time.

## 💡 Daily Tips Page

This is your home screen—where your sustainable journey happens!

### What You'll See

#### Today's Eco-Tip Card
A beautiful card showing:
- **The tip text** - A simple action you can take today (e.g., "Turn off your monitor when stepping away for more than 5 minutes")
- **Category tag** - Color-coded category (Energy Saving, Device Care, Disposal, or Eco-Buying)
- **"Why it matters" button** - Tap to see a brief explanation of the tip's environmental impact
- **"Mark as Done" button** - Complete the tip to build your streak!

#### Tip Categories
Tips are color-coded by category:

- **🔋 Energy Saving** (Blue) - Save power, reduce your electricity bill, and cut carbon emissions
- **📱 Device Care** (Green) - Maintain your electronics to make them last longer
- **♻️ Responsible Disposal** (Orange) - Learn where and how to recycle or donate old devices
- **🛒 Eco-Buying** (Purple) - Make sustainable choices when purchasing new electronics

### How to Use Daily Tips

1. **Read your daily tip** - Take a moment to understand the action
2. **Tap "Why it matters"** - See the environmental impact (available for most tips)
3. **Do the action!** - It usually takes less than 5 minutes
4. **Mark as done** - Tap the button to complete today's tip
5. **Come back tomorrow** - A new tip will be waiting for you!

### Special Features

- **Pull to refresh** - Swipe down if you want to see a different tip (won't affect your streak)
- **Automatic daily rotation** - Tips change automatically every day
- **Smart explanations** - Most tips include a short explanation of why they matter

## 📊 Progress Tracking

Track your eco-journey and see your impact grow over time!

### What You'll See

#### 🎯 Weekly Progress Ring
A circular progress indicator showing:
- **Percentage complete** - How many days this week you've completed tips
- **Days completed** - Visual display of which days you marked tips as done
- **Weekly goal** - Complete all 7 days for maximum impact!

**How it works:**
- The ring fills up as you complete more days
- Green checkmarks show completed days
- Gray dots show incomplete days
- Resets every Sunday to start fresh

#### 🔥 Current Streak
Your **streak** is the number of consecutive days you've completed tips.

- **Daily completion** - Do a tip every day to keep your streak alive!
- **Streak counter** - Big number showing your current streak
- **Motivation boost** - Watch it grow and stay motivated!

**Pro tip:** Enable notifications so you never miss a day!

#### 📅 30-Day History Heatmap
A calendar view showing your last 30 days of activity:
- **Green squares** - Days you completed a tip ✅
- **Gray squares** - Days you missed 
- **Visual patterns** - See your consistency at a glance

This helps you:
- Spot patterns in your eco-habits
- Stay accountable to your goals
- Celebrate your progress visually

#### 📈 Recent Completions
A list of your recent tip completions showing:
- Number of tips completed in the last 30 days
- Your consistency percentage
- Quick stats about your eco-impact

### Clear Progress
If you ever want to start fresh, you can:
1. Scroll to the bottom of the Progress page
2. Tap **"Clear Progress"**
3. Confirm your choice
4. All streaks and history will be reset

**Note:** This action cannot be undone, so use it carefully!

## ♻️ Recycling & Trade-In Directory

Find trusted places to responsibly recycle, trade-in, or donate your old electronics.

### What You'll Find

The directory is organized into three sections:

#### 🇵🇭 Local (Philippines)
Recycling centers and programs in major Philippine cities:
- **Manila Recycling Center** - Metro Manila community center with free pickup
- **Quezon City E-Waste Collection** - Quarterly collection drives and barangay drop-off points
- **Makati City EcoHub** - Environmental hub with repair cafes and refurbishment
- **Envirocycle Davao** - Davao-based professional e-waste recycling service

#### 🏢 National Programs
Nationwide initiatives you can access anywhere in the Philippines:
- **Globe E-Waste Zero** - Drop-off bins in malls and partner locations nationwide
- **DENR E-Waste Management** - Government oversight and collection drive schedules
- **SM Cares E-Waste Collection** - Bins at SM Supermalls across the country
- **Ayala Malls Green Initiatives** - Collection points at Ayala mall locations
- **PLDT-Smart E-Waste Program** - Mobile phone recycling at retail stores

#### 🌏 International Resources
Global programs and major brand recycling initiatives:
- **Best Buy Recycling** (USA) - Free electronics recycling at all US stores
- **Apple Trade In** - Global trade-in and recycling for Apple devices
- **Dell Reconnect** - Free computer recycling via Goodwill partnership
- **e-Stewards Certified** - Global network of certified responsible recyclers
- **Earth911** - US-based recycling location finder
- **Google Sustainability** - Pixel and Chromebook recycling programs
- **ERI & Sims Lifecycle** - Professional enterprise e-waste services

### How to Use the Directory

1. **Browse by category** - Local, National, or International
2. **Read descriptions** - Learn what each program accepts and offers
3. **Tap "Open"** - Opens the program's website in your browser
4. **Find nearest location** - Most programs have location finders on their sites
5. **Prepare your e-waste** - Remove personal data before drop-off (see tips!)

### Before You Recycle Checklist
- ✅ Back up important data
- ✅ Factory reset devices to erase personal information
- ✅ Remove SIM cards and memory cards
- ✅ Remove batteries if required by the recycling program
- ✅ Bundle cables and accessories together

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Is GreenWise free?**  
A: Yes! GreenWise is completely free with no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

**Q: Do I need internet to use GreenWise?**  
A: No! All tips work offline. You only need internet to open links in the Recycling Directory.

**Q: Does GreenWise work on iPhone?**  
A: Currently, GreenWise is available for Android only (version 7.0 and higher). iOS support may come in the future.

**Q: How much storage does the app use?**  
A: Approximately 18-20 MB—about the size of 5-6 photos.

### Tips & Progress

**Q: Why only one tip per day?**  
A: Research shows that small, daily habits are more effective than trying to change everything at once. One tip per day keeps it simple and builds lasting habits.

**Q: What if I miss a day?**  
A: Your streak will reset, but don't worry! Just start again tomorrow. Every action counts, even if you're not on a streak.

**Q: Can I see more than one tip per day?**  
A: Yes! Pull down on the Daily Tips screen to refresh and see a different tip. This won't affect your "Mark as Done" for the day.

**Q: Do tips repeat?**  
A: With 365 unique tips, they rotate yearly. You'll see each tip approximately once per year.

**Q: What happens to my progress if I uninstall the app?**  
A: Your progress data will be lost if you uninstall. Make sure you're okay with resetting before uninstalling!

### Features

**Q: How do notifications work?**  
A: If you enable notifications, GreenWise will send you a daily reminder to complete your eco-tip. You can set your preferred time during first launch.

**Q: What's the difference between the tip categories?**  
A: 
- **Energy Saving** = Reduce power consumption
- **Device Care** = Make electronics last longer  
- **Disposal** = Recycle or donate responsibly
- **Eco-Buying** = Choose sustainable products

**Q: Are the recycling locations verified?**  
A: Yes! All programs listed in the Recycling Directory are established organizations, government programs, or certified recyclers. Always check their websites for current details.

### Technical

**Q: Why does the app need notification permissions?**  
A: Only for daily reminders. You can skip this permission, but you won't get reminder notifications.

**Q: Does GreenWise drain my battery?**  
A: No! GreenWise uses minimal background resources and follows Android battery optimization best practices.

**Q: Can I use GreenWise on a tablet?**  
A: Yes! GreenWise works on Android tablets running version 7.0 or higher.

**Q: Is my data safe?**  
A: Absolutely! All data is stored locally on your device. GreenWise doesn't send any information to external servers.

## 💪 Tips for Success

### Build Your Eco-Habit

1. **Enable notifications** - Daily reminders keep you consistent
2. **Check in at the same time each day** - Make it part of your morning or evening routine
