# GreenWise – Presentation Overview

A simple guide to explain what GreenWise is, why it matters, and how each page helps users live a more sustainable digital life.

## 1) What is GreenWise?
GreenWise is a friendly companion app that helps people build small, daily habits for sustainable electronics use. It gives one helpful tip per day, explains the "why" in plain language, tracks your progress, and points you to real recycling/trade‑in options.

- Audience: students, households, teachers, and professionals
- Platforms: Android, iOS, Web, Desktop (Flutter)
- Core values: simple, actionable, rewarding

## 2) Why it matters (Problem)
Electronic waste (e‑waste) is one of the fastest‑growing waste streams worldwide. Many people don’t know how to care for devices, when to repair or trade‑in, or where to recycle. Simple daily actions—charging smartly, maintaining devices, disposing properly—add up to big impact.

## 3) How GreenWise helps (Solution)
- Bite‑sized, daily eco‑tips focused on electronics
- Clear, concise explanations of environmental impact (when available)
- Habit tracking (streaks, weekly completion) that motivates
- Real‑world recycling & trade‑in resources

---

## 4) Pages and their purpose

### A. Onboarding
- Purpose: Welcome users and set the tone. Introduces the app’s mission and how it works.
- Importance: First impression; reduces drop‑off with a clean, green visual and subtle particles.
- Impact: Users quickly understand the value and continue to the daily tips.

What they see:
- A few simple screens, eco‑leaf particle background, and clickable dots to navigate.

---

### B. Daily Tip (Home)
- Purpose: Deliver one actionable eco‑tip each day for electronics: device care, energy saving, disposal, and eco‑buying.
- Importance: Keeps the journey simple and consistent—one step at a time.
- Impact: Users build habits they can actually keep, reducing energy waste and extending device life.

What they can do:
- Read “Today’s Eco‑Tip” in a friendly card.
- Optionally read “Why it matters” for a short explanation.
- Tap “Done today” to record completion (feeds streak/progress).

---

### C. Progress
- Purpose: Show momentum and celebrate consistency.
- Importance: Visual feedback keeps people motivated.
- Impact: Streaks and weekly completion help turn actions into sustainable routines.

What they see:
- Weekly ring (how you’re doing this week)
- Streak stats (current and longest)
- 30‑day heatmap of completions
- Clear Progress button (with confirmation)

---

### D. Recycle & Trade‑in Directory
- Purpose: Connect users to real‑world solutions for responsible disposal and upgrades.
- Importance: Bridges knowledge to action—where to bring or trade devices.
- Impact: More electronics get recycled or re‑used; fewer end up in landfills.

What they get (examples):
- Philippines: Globe E‑Waste Zero, DENR resources, Envirocycle, E‑Dispo, SM Cares events
- Global: ERI, Sims Lifecycle Services, ATRenew, PCs for People, Best Buy Recycling, Apple Trade‑In, Dell Reconnect, Google Sustainability
- Each entry is a card with an “Open” button to launch the official website in the browser.

---

### E. Settings
- Purpose: Put users in control of themes, motion, and preferences.
- Importance: Accessibility and comfort matter for daily adoption.
- Impact: Reduce Motion helps sensitive users; dark mode improves readability and battery life.

What they can adjust:
- Dark Mode
- Reduce Motion (less animation)
- Future: categories, notifications, language

---

## 5) The app’s overall impact
- Environmental: Lower energy waste, longer‑lasting devices, more proper recycling
- Educational: Everyday learning through short, understandable explanations
- Behavioral: Habit formation via streaks and weekly rhythm

## 6) Demo flow (2–3 minutes)
1. Show Onboarding (1 screen) → proceed
2. Open Daily Tip → tap “Why it matters” → mark “Done today”
3. Go to Progress → weekly ring + streaks + heatmap
4. Open Recycle & Trade‑in → tap “Open” on a PH or global card
5. Visit Settings → toggle Dark Mode and Reduce Motion

## 7) One‑liner to close
“GreenWise turns small, clear actions into everyday sustainable habits for your digital life.”
